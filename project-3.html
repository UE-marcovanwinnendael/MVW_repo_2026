Concept

What if sports had a pilot episode?

Behind Sports was developed together with my colleagues Enzo Carreira and Duarte Bernardes. We created a pilot episode as a proof of concept for an AR sports experience that puts viewers inside the story. The first episode centres on Formula 1, using augmented reality to overlay race data, 3D assets and immersive environments onto the real world.

The experience was developed from initial concepts and brand identity in Figma to a fully modelled Formula 1 environment and a detailed V6 hybrid engine in Blender, then assembled and brought to life in Unity. Every asset is original.

The final render showcases a cinematic Formula 1 briefing room with curved screens, live dashboards, race footage and a Ferrari in the foreground. This is what sports content could look like.

Immersive F1 Environment
A cinematic briefing room with curved screens, race footage, live telemetry dashboards and a full size Ferrari Formula 1 car, built from scratch in Blender and Unity.
V6 Engine: Hand Modelled
A detailed V6 hybrid engine modelled entirely by hand in Blender. Every component, including the cylinders, turbos, wiring and motor generator unit, was built from reference to showcase the engineering behind F1.
Live Data Dashboards
Custom designed telemetry screens showing lap times, tyre strategies, speed traces and driver comparisons, all styled within the Behind Sports brand system.

Limitations

The university had promised to arrange a green screen studio for our production, but the studio was not made available. As a result, we could not record ourselves as planned and had to use a placeholder presenter in the final video. This affected how closely the pilot episode could reflect our original concept.
