01 / Concept

What if finding a pet sitter felt as trustworthy as asking a neighbour?

A regional marketplace with a personal approach.

Most pet care platforms are designed to work everywhere. Patinha focuses on Greater Lisbon, where language, neighbourhoods, trust and local expectations matter.

I designed and built the project independently, from its visual identity and interface to database configuration, authentication, the booking flow, payment integration and administration tools.

The application uses a lightweight JavaScript frontend and is deployed on Vercel. The product includes Portuguese and English interfaces and dedicated terms and privacy information.

02 / Experience

Three connected experiences.

Each feature was designed as part of an end to end workflow for owners, sitters and the person managing the platform.

01 / DISCOVERY

A map that respects privacy

Owners explore profiles through an interactive Lisbon map, search and filter by care type. Public locations are deliberately approximate, helping protect sitters' home addresses.

02 / BOOKINGS

From request to payment

The booking flow connects owner requests and sitter responses with Stripe Checkout. Payment events feed into booking updates, with a separate payout flow designed around completed stays.

03 / COMMUNITY

Trust, conversation and reviews

Sitters go through an approval process before appearing publicly. The product combines chat, media sharing, booking updates and reviews to help both sides make informed choices.
