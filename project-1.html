Overview

Walk with your dog freely — anywhere in the city.

Off The Leash is a platform designed for dog owners who want to find safe, legal places for their dogs to run, play and burn energy. The app combines a live 3D map interface with crowd-sourced community data and filtered search results.

Built in Unreal Engine with real API data integration, the platform pulls live geospatial information on off-leash zones, trails and parks across metropolitan Lisbon — and presents it in a clean, interactive 3D environment.

Location Coverage

Metropolitan Lisbon — this is the area we work with

The app focuses on the greater Lisbon metropolitan area — covering Sintra, Cascais, Lisbon, Almada and Sesimbra. The region's diverse landscape, from coastal trails to urban parks, makes it a perfect environment for off-leash exploration.

All geodata is sourced through live API calls to ensure the information stays current — no stale databases, no outdated park boundaries.
