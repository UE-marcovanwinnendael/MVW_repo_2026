Overview

A virtual darkroom you have to experience to believe.

The Darkroom Secret is a fully immersive VR escape room set inside a detailed recreation of the real analog photo printing lab at our university. Players solve puzzles inspired by authentic darkroom chemistry and techniques to escape. The result is both a game and an educational experience.

I created The Darkroom Secret together with my colleagues Enzo Carreira and Duarte Bernardes. As a team, we conceived, designed and built the experience from scratch. Every object in the scene, from the ceiling lights to the chemical trays, was modelled, textured and placed by hand. Nothing was sourced from asset stores.

Environment Design

A 1:1 replica of a real university darkroom

We visited the actual photo printing lab to measure, photograph and document its details before rebuilding it from scratch in Blender. The result is a virtual space that feels genuinely familiar to anyone who has been in that room.

From the industrial pendant lamps to the worktops and chemical sinks, every element was modelled and textured by hand. Substance Painter was used to bring authentic wear, grime and material variation to each surface.

Hero Asset

The Axomat 5a enlarger, modelled in Blender

The centrepiece of the escape room is a fully modelled Axomat 5a colour enlarger. Every component, including the bellows, colour filter head with its C/M/Y/D dials, column, baseboard and timer dial, was built individually using reference photos of the real machine.

Textures were crafted in Substance Painter to replicate the matte black casing, metallic highlights, and the subtle wear marks of a machine that has been used for decades in a teaching environment.
