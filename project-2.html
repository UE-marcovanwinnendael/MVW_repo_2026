The trailer

A crazy journey

Every step is packed with the best of what silliness and humor can do.

Twister rushes into IADE, late and with no idea where the class is. The elevators are out of order, so the only way is up the stairs. Each floor throws a new challenge, surreal twist or nostalgic reference at them, all taken from real life in the building.

The journey ends with a message: every step leads to new beginnings, as the old building turns into the new campus.

Meet…
Twister

Your silly, clumsy but lovable, charismatic new friend.

Twister is a student at IADE with their head in the clouds and a talent for tripping. They are genderless, and the name was picked to carry the feeling of confusion and messiness the animation brings. Anyone should be able to see a bit of themselves in Twister.

Twister wears IADE’s typical Tuna outfit, with its kilt, a typical dress in Portugal. The pan on the head is a nod to the ontgroening (hazing rituals) that new students go through.

Typography

Nudgewink is the playful, slightly misaligned main typeface. Impact works where space is narrow, since it is tall. SFT Schrifted Sans is for long, readable text.

Logo rules

Only recommended color pairings, never stretched, flipped, with effects or with gradients. The question mark sets the spacing.

Projection tests

We tested images on two projectors and calibrated for the weaker one. Light yellows read as white, similar shades blur together, and bright colors need strong contrast.

Projection mapping

3 projectors. 1 uneven surface. 2 videos at once.

WTF was first meant for the entrance of the university. That turned out not to be possible, so we projected onto a 1:2 scale model of the real elevator doors, almost identical in shape to the real thing.

The difficulty was designing and animating for such a strange format. We unwrapped the 3D model and built a UV template in Figma, and since the doors are dark, we used bright colors so the light would not be absorbed.

The piece is actually two videos playing at the same time: one for the elevator wall and one for the box in the corner, which represents the IADE building. The box is a hollow cuboid made from 3 mm foam board, 550 × 250 × 100 mm.

  • 3 projectors
  • 2 simultaneous videos
  • 1:2 scale elevator model
  • Tested on the 3rd floor of IADE

The poster

Twister on the run, floor by floor.

The poster follows the same rules as the projection: the stair-step title, bold flat color blocks and the elevator doors Twister keeps missing.

Promotional campaign

Quirky but professional, playful but strategic.

To promote the videomapping show we built a campaign that connects everything we made. It aims at IADE students, the wider IADE community (alumni and staff) and the creative scene, and it teases the story just enough to build curiosity.
